Core Scam Vectors Explained

Common psychological manipulation tactics used by online scammers

⏰ Artificial Urgency

Scammers demand immediate action within hours to trigger panic and prevent you from consulting friends or official channels.

🔑 OTP Solicitations

One-time passwords are the final barrier protecting your accounts. Legitimate banks and service providers will never ask you to forward an OTP.

🔗 Obfuscated Links

Shortened URLs (bit.ly, tinyurl) mask malicious destination domains. Even HTTPS websites can be fraudulent—HTTPS only encrypts connection transit.

💼 Upfront Advance Fees

Whether disguised as a lottery processing charge, courier fee, or job registration fee, legitimate organizations never demand payment to release your money.
