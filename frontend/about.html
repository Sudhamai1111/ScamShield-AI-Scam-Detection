01 / The purpose

Scams exploit trust. We make warning signs visible.

Scam messages often rely on urgency, impersonation, fear, attractive rewards or requests for sensitive information. ScamShield turns those signals into an understandable assessment instead of presenting an unexplained label.

What problem are we solving?

People receive suspicious SMS messages, WhatsApp forwards, emails, fake job offers and payment requests every day. It can be difficult to distinguish a genuine notification from social engineering, especially when a message pressures the recipient to act immediately.

Our approach: identify observable indicators, combine them into a bounded risk score, explain why they matter, and suggest safer next steps. The result is decision support—not a guarantee that a message is fraudulent or genuine.

03 / Product capabilities

More than a red or green label.

Each module has a distinct role in helping users inspect messages and understand community-level scam patterns.

🎯

Risk assessment

A score from 0 to 100 groups the observed signals into Low Risk, Needs Attention, Suspicious or High Risk bands.

🧩

Explainable indicators

Detected categories and matched evidence help users understand which message features contributed to the assessment.

🔎

Link inspection

Checks URL characteristics such as protocol, common shorteners, IP-based hosts and selected suspicious TLDs.

📝

Community reports

Users can submit scam encounters to support awareness. Anonymous sender details are masked in public report output.

📊

Threat statistics

Aggregated reports are summarized by category and platform to show patterns in submitted incidents.

💾

Flexible storage

Firestore can be configured for cloud storage. Without credentials, the application uses a local JSON fallback.

04 / Stay protected

Six habits that make digital life safer.

ScamShield supports awareness, but good security habits remain the first line of defence.

TIP 01

Keep OTPs private

Never disclose an OTP, password, PIN or recovery code to someone who contacts you unexpectedly.

TIP 02

Verify payment requests

Never enter a UPI PIN to receive money. A UPI PIN authorizes a payment from your account.

TIP 03

Inspect links carefully

HTTPS encrypts a connection; it does not prove that a website or its owner is trustworthy.

TIP 04

Question upfront fees

Be cautious of job offers that demand deposits, registration charges or payment to unlock earnings.

TIP 05

Be sceptical of guarantees

Promises of unusually high, risk-free returns or instant rewards deserve independent verification.

TIP 06

Verify the sender independently

Contact banks, companies or authorities through an official website or known phone number—not the message link.

Important limitation

ScamShield provides an automated risk assessment based on detected indicators. It cannot guarantee whether a message is genuine or fraudulent. Verify important requests independently through official channels.
